    Follow the flag

    Send a clue associated with the target. The server ranks all words by their association with your clue, placing the closest at the bottom.

    In casual arcade, the board holds 20 phrases and the bottom three positions form the clear zone. Your target can rank first, second, or third to clear for 100 points. In casual arcade, five consecutive successful clues sweep all words in the clear zone. The stack refills the zone, and each new target also clears while it remains in range and the same clue is legal. Each removed word earns 100 points. A miss or invalid clue resets the streak; service failures reset it too. Event games keep target-only clears. New words keep arriving; a full stack ends the game.

    Casual arrivals speed up every 500 points, from five seconds down to two seconds between phrases. Event games and recorded test sequences use their own fixed settings.

    The clock keeps running while your clue is ranked and while you reconnect.
