    Game details

    Follow the flag

    Send a clue associated with the target. The server ranks all words by their association with your clue, placing the closest at the bottom.

    1. Find an association.Use a clue like “orbit” for the target “moon”. Avoid the target itself or its word forms.
    2. Bring the flag into range.The closest words move to the bottom. In casual arcade, land the target in positions 1–3 to clear it for 100 points.
    3. Keep room in the stack.New words arrive as you play. A full, 20-word stack ends the game.
    Streaks, pace, and other modes

    Five consecutive successful clues sweep the clear zone. Surviving words move down, and each new target also clears while it is in range and the same clue is legal. Each removed word earns 100 points.

    A miss, invalid clue, or service failure resets the streak. Casual arrivals speed up every 500 points, from five seconds down to two.

    Event games keep target-only clears. Events use their own fixed schedules.

    In live games, the clock keeps running while a clue is ranked or the connection recovers. The guided warm-up is untimed.
